## Design notes

Original application notes; consult Apple for the full guidance.

- Use material to communicate interface layering and hierarchy, with clear separation between content and controls.
- Consult current Liquid Glass guidance for placement and variants; evaluate contrast and avoid applying the effect indiscriminately.
